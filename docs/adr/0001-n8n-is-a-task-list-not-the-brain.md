# 0001. n8n is a task list, not the brain

## Context
A prospecting agent needs a clock, inbox reading, email sending, and notices. n8n does all of that well. The temptation is to also encode the tactics there: whom to contact, what to write, when to insist.

## Decision
n8n wakes the bot and delivers the task and the context. The bot is the one that decides the text, the time, and the angle, and it may also not execute a task. Every decision (executou, adiou, recusou, with a reason) is recorded in `crm.tarefas` and shows up on the board. The rules that can be verified (cap, interval, round, window) stay in the API.

## Consequences
- Changing the agent or the model does not require touching the workflows.
- The workflows are short and become JSON generated by `n8n/gerar_workflows.py`, with a readable diff review.
- The audit does not depend on n8n's execution history.
- Tactics lose the predictability of a flow designed node by node; in exchange they gain adaptation to each lead's context.
